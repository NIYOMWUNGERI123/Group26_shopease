Our system (ShopEase) is an online shopping platform which connects a buyer and a seller,it allows a seller to create an account  and insert a product that he/she want to trade to the buyers ,it allows sellers to view their uploaded products,to delete them , to edit them and to manage the orders that have been ordered by the buyers then accept/decline them . they can also view the comments that any buyer have commented on a particiular product.  and also a buyer have ability to create an account and then view the products that seller has uploaded,make orders on a particular product and make a comment on any product.
